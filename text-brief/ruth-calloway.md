# Ruth Calloway: card lines as they stand

78 cards, upright and reversed. "Means" is the card's standard meaning in the app, which every reader's line must agree with. Ruth has no sign-off. The rules and the changes wanted are in `00-brief.md`.

## The Fool (`maj_00`)
- Means, upright: A beginning that asks for trust rather than a plan. Step into the unknown with an open mind, and travel light. (new beginnings, innocence, a leap of faith, spontaneity, potential)
- Means, reversed: Either you are leaping without looking, or you are too afraid to take a step you need to take. Check your footing, then go. (recklessness, fear of the unknown, naivety, hesitation, carelessness)

**upright:** Rookie in the driver's seat, full tank, no route planned — and somehow that's the play. Pull out, hon. The road teaches what the map can't.

**reversed:** You're idling in the lot scared to merge. Or worse, you pulled out without checking your mirrors. Either way, quit stalling or slow down — pick one.

## The Magician (`maj_01`)
- Means, upright: You have the tools and the will to make something real. Focus your attention and act. (manifestation, willpower, resourcefulness, skill, focused action)
- Means, reversed: Skill without direction, or talent spent on appearances and tricks. Check whether you are deceiving others or yourself. (manipulation, untapped potential, deception, scattered focus, illusion)

**upright:** Every gauge reads full — fuel, oil, brakes, you. You've got everything in this cab to make the haul. Turn the key.

**reversed:** Lot of chrome, no diesel. You're talking a big rig and driving a wagon. Check what's actually under the hood before you promise the load.

**reversed:** You've been running on gut alone with your eyes closed. Time to open 'em — some of that quiet was you avoiding the gauges.
