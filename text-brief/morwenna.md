# Madame Morwenna Ravenscroft: card lines as they stand

78 cards, upright and reversed. "Means" is the card's standard meaning in the app, which every reader's line must agree with. Each card has one proverb, used both ways up. The rules and the changes wanted are in `00-brief.md`.

## The Fool (`maj_00`)
- Means, upright: A beginning that asks for trust rather than a plan. Step into the unknown with an open mind, and travel light. (new beginnings, innocence, a leap of faith, spontaneity, potential)
- Means, reversed: Either you are leaping without looking, or you are too afraid to take a step you need to take. Check your footing, then go. (recklessness, fear of the unknown, naivety, hesitation, carelessness)

**upright:** Look at him—tiptoeing along the precipice with his heart in his hand and daylight in his marrow. The Fool does not leap because he is reckless; he leaps because the ground behind him has turned to ash. Step forward, seeker. The universe builds the bridge the moment your heel leaves the cliff edge.

**reversed:** Ah, the Fool stumbling backwards in terror, or lunging blindfolded into a ravine out of sheer petulance. You are either clinging to a cage because the door feels too wide, or sprinting without checking if there is water in the well. Open your eyes before your collarbone pays the price.

**proverb:** Grace and disaster share the same stepping stone; intention decides which foot you plant.

## The Magician (`maj_01`)
- Means, upright: You have the tools and the will to make something real. Focus your attention and act. (manifestation, willpower, resourcefulness, skill, focused action)
- Means, reversed: Skill without direction, or talent spent on appearances and tricks. Check whether you are deceiving others or yourself. (manipulation, untapped potential, deception, scattered focus, illusion)

**upright:** Four tools upon the velvet cloth: wood, water, iron, and gold. You sit here asking if you have what it takes, yet your workbench is already crowded with miracles waiting for hands. What you call lack is merely hesitation in disguise. Speak your decree into the quiet; the aether is listening.

**reversed:** A parlor illusionist playing three-card monte with his own soul. Clever tongues, glittering promises, and nothing in the cupboard. Beware where your eloquence outpaces your integrity, or where you allow another's silver rhetoric to blind you to the dagger up their sleeve.

**proverb:** A spell without consequence is only theater; do not summon what you cannot command.

## The High Priestess (`maj_02`)
- Means, upright: Trust what you already know but have not said aloud. Sit with the question instead of asking the room. (intuition, mystery, the subconscious, inner knowing, stillness)
- Means, reversed: You are ignoring your intuition, keeping secrets, or settling for surface answers. Listen to what you have been drowning out. (ignored instinct, secrets, superficiality, detachment, hidden motives)

**upright:** Hush. Malachi has ceased his squawking, and so should you. The Priestess sits between the dark pillar and the pale, keeping secrets older than your lineage. What you seek is not written in ink; it is beating in the hush beneath your ribs. Do not ask for advice when your blood already gave you the answer three midnights ago.

**reversed:** You are suffocating your intuition beneath a mound of cold logic and parlor gossip. Why do you pretend not to hear the knock at the cellar door? Silence is not empty, darling; it is pregnant with what you are avoiding.

**proverb:** The truth never shouts; she simply waits until your clamor runs out of breath.

## The Empress (`maj_03`)
- Means, upright: Growth, nurture, and abundance. Feed what you are growing, whether a project, a relationship, or yourself, and let it ripen. (abundance, fertility, creativity, nurturing, pleasure)
- Means, reversed: Creative block, dependence, smothering, or neglect of your own needs. Tend your own garden first. (creative block, depletion, smothering, neglect, overindulgence)

**upright:** A velvet gown heavy with wheat and honey. The Empress does not scrape or bargain; she blooms where she sits because she remembers the earth belongs to the tender. Nourishment is arriving—in your craft, your flesh, or your home. Stop treating rest like a stolen misdemeanor.

**reversed:** Smothering soil or withered branches. Either you are pouring yourself into broken jars until your well runs dry, or you have grown vain, demanding adoration without tending the hearth. You cannot harvest what you refuse to water.

**proverb:** The soil cares nothing for your hurried anxieties; fruit ripens only when it is done.

## The Emperor (`maj_04`)
- Means, upright: Structure, authority, and discipline. Set the rules and boundaries that let the work stand up. (authority, structure, stability, discipline, leadership)
- Means, reversed: Control has become rigid or tyrannical, or there is no structure at all. Ask whether firmness has become stubbornness. (tyranny, rigidity, chaos, micromanagement, loss of control)

**upright:** Cold granite, carved armrests, and a gaze that measures kingdoms. The Emperor demands order where you have tolerated chaos. Build your boundary, lock the gate, and stop apologizing for possessing authority over your own estate. Sovereignty is not tyranny; it is stewardship.

**reversed:** A brittle tyrant screaming at shadows, or a ruler who has let the moat fill with mud. Rigidity has calcified into cruelty, or spineless surrender has invited thieves into the throne room. Reclaim your spine before someone else commands your knees.

**proverb:** A castle without stone walls is just a monument to wishful thinking.

## The Hierophant (`maj_05`)
- Means, upright: Tradition, teaching, and shared belief. Learn the way this has been done before you decide to do it differently. (tradition, mentorship, wisdom, institutions, shared belief)
- Means, reversed: Dogma, hypocrisy, or rebellion against rules that no longer fit. Question the form, but do not reject it out of spite. (dogma, rebellion, unconventional paths, hypocrisy, conformity)

**upright:** The smell of ancient hymnals and gilded keystones. The Hierophant stands at the threshold of tradition and lineage. There are rules carved into the stone that survived ten thousand storms. Learn the catechism, seek the master, or find sanctuary in shared ritual. You need not invent the wheel in the dark.

**reversed:** Dogma turned to rot. The temple elders are counting tithes while the chapel ceiling leaks. Break the stained glass if it keeps out the sky, seeker. Orthodoxy has ceased serving the divine and now serves only the warden.

**proverb:** Tradition is carrying the fire, not worshiping the cold ashes.

## The Lovers (`maj_06`)
- Means, upright: A union, or a choice about what you value. Choose what you can live with, not only what you feel. (union, harmony, shared values, connection, choice)
- Means, reversed: Misaligned values, a divided heart, or a choice you keep refusing to make. You cannot walk two roads at once. (disharmony, misalignment, conflict, indecision, broken bonds)

**upright:** Twin fires dancing on a single wick. This is not merely romance; it is the holy terror of choosing who you become when another's mirror is held before your face. A covenant of values beckons. What you pledge yourself to here will stitch itself into your fate for seven seasons.

**reversed:** Discord in the bedchamber and treason in the heart. A choice made to appease fear rather than honor. You are either loving a ghost of your own invention or betraying your truest marrow to avoid dining alone.

**proverb:** Love is an altar, not a bargaining counter; place nothing there you intend to snatch back.

## The Chariot (`maj_07`)
- Means, upright: Victory through will and focus. Hold the opposing pulls together and steer. (determination, willpower, victory, control, focus)
- Means, reversed: Loss of direction, aggression, or force without a goal. Fix what is steering before you push harder. (loss of control, aggression, obstacles, aimlessness, a hard stop)

**upright:** Black steed and white steed, snarling in their harness, but your hands hold the reins. The Chariot does not wait for smooth roads; it cuts its own rut through the bog. Iron focus, unrelenting momentum. Fix your eyes upon the gate and drive hard.

**reversed:** The wheels have sheared off in the mud, or the horses are dragging you through the thorns by your boots. Aggression without discipline is only a louder kind of suicide. Pull back on the bit before the carriage rolls.

**proverb:** Momentum without steering is merely an accident in a hurry.

## Strength (`maj_08`)
- Means, upright: Courage that does not need to shout. Meet what is fierce in you or around you with patience and a steady hand. (inner strength, compassion, patience, courage, calm)
- Means, reversed: Self-doubt, temper, or fear you are trying to force down. Real strength is quiet; find it again. (self-doubt, aggression, impatience, weakness, burnout)

**upright:** Not the club of Hercules, but the soft, unyielding palm pressed against the beast's velvet jaw. The lion roars, and the maiden smiles. You will conquer this storm not by fury, but by quiet endurance, patience, and a compassion so stubborn it shames violence.

**reversed:** Raw fury gnashing its teeth, or self-doubt whimpering in the shadows. You are either letting your lower impulses drive the carriage, or cowering before a challenge that would yield if you simply stood upright and breathed.

**proverb:** The hand that soothes the predator holds far more power than the blade that slays it.

## The Hermit (`maj_09`)
- Means, upright: Solitude and reflection. Step away from the noise to find your own answer. (introspection, solitude, guidance, searching, discernment)
- Means, reversed: Isolation, loneliness, or refusing the quiet because it would tell you something. Come back far enough to be reached, or go far enough to hear yourself. (isolation, loneliness, withdrawal, paranoia, feeling lost)

**upright:** A solitary lantern swinging in the mountain gale. Step away from the salon, querent. The chatter of the crowd is polluting your judgment. Go up the winding stairs into your own solitude; the lantern you carry only illuminates one step at a time, but one step is all you are asked to walk.

**reversed:** Solitude curdled into sour isolation, or cowardice masquerading as wisdom. You have hidden in your cave for so long that your eyes water at daylight. It is time to bring the lantern down the mountain before you freeze in your own righteousness.

**proverb:** The lamp was given to light the road, not to inspect your own navel forever.

## Wheel of Fortune (`maj_10`)
- Means, upright: A turn of fortune, a change of season. Something stuck is moving; ride it rather than resisting it. (cycles, change, good fortune, turning points, luck)
- Means, reversed: Bad luck, resistance to change, or a cycle you keep repeating. You cannot wind the wheel back; work with where it is. (misfortune, resistance to change, stagnation, bad timing, disruption)

**upright:** The great brass wheel turns, whether the beggar weeps or the king feasts. A sudden turn of destiny is at hand. What was low rises; what was triumphant descends. Do not cling to the rim where the dizziness lives—move inward to the still axle.

**reversed:** A run of ill luck or the frantic attempt to jam a stick into the spinning gears. Misfortune is not personal vengeance from the stars; it is simply the wheel descending. Endure the dip; the ascent is forged in how gracefully you weather the trough.

**proverb:** The wheel cares nothing for your title; it turns because turning is its nature.

## Justice (`maj_11`)
- Means, upright: Cause and effect, fairness, and accountability. What was done will be weighed; be honest about your part. (truth, fairness, cause and effect, integrity, accountability)
- Means, reversed: Unfairness, bias, dishonesty, or avoiding responsibility. Check whose thumb is on the scale. (injustice, bias, dishonesty, evasion, conflict)

**upright:** The double-edged sword poised above the brass pans. No tears, no excuses, no theatrics. Truth cuts cleanly through sentimentality. You will receive exactly what you have sown, down to the last ounce of barley. Settle your debts, speak without embroidery, and stand tall before the scales.

**reversed:** Biased arbitration, rationalized dishonesties, or fleeing from consequences. You may fool the magistrate in the wig, darling, but the cosmic ledgers do not misplace an entry. Confess the deficit before the bailiff arrives.

**proverb:** The scale does not negotiate; it simply reports the weight of your choices.

## The Hanged Man (`maj_12`)
- Means, upright: A pause, a surrender, a new angle. Stop forcing the outcome and let the view change. (surrender, new perspective, pause, letting go, suspension)
- Means, reversed: Stalling, martyrdom, or resistance disguised as patience. Letting go is not the same as doing nothing. (martyrdom, stagnation, resistance, indecision, delay)

**upright:** Suspended by the ankle from the living tree, yet a halo gleams around his brow. Surrender your frantic thrashing. When every door is locked, stop kicking the oak—turn your vision upside down. What looks like defeat to the crowd is your quiet initiation into wisdom.

**reversed:** Martyrdom performed for an audience, or stubborn stalling disguised as patience. You are dangling there complaining of the headache while holding the rope yourself. Cut the cord and stand on your feet.

**proverb:** Surrender is not giving up; it is giving over what you never truly controlled.

## Death (`maj_13`)
- Means, upright: An ending that makes room. Let what is finished be finished so something else can begin. (transformation, endings, release, transition, renewal)
- Means, reversed: Fear of change, clinging to what is already over, or an ending dragged out past its time. Let it end. (clinging to the past, fear of change, stagnation, prolonged endings, resistance)

**upright:** Do not flinch. Malachi bows his head for this one. Death is the black plow that turns the exhausted field so spring can breathe. What is dying in your life has already ceased its pulse; stop trying to perform CPR on a corpse. Lay it in the crypt, weep your tears, and clear the room for what must be born.

**reversed:** Clinging with fingernails to what has already turned to formaldehyde. Decaying habits, dead partnerships, lingering specters. Holding on will not resurrect the dead; it will only poison the living.

**proverb:** The scythe is merciful; it cuts down only what has finished its season.

## Temperance (`maj_14`)
- Means, upright: Balance, patience, and moderation. Blend the opposites rather than choosing one. (balance, moderation, patience, harmony, integration)
- Means, reversed: Excess, imbalance, or forcing together things that do not mix. Restore the middle before you continue. (imbalance, excess, discord, impatience, extremes)

**upright:** The winged angel pouring water and fire between two golden urns without spilling a droplet. Alchemy of the middle way. You must blend the opposites within your breast—the fury and the mercy, the ambition and the patience. Moderation is not blandness; it is mastery of the brew.

**reversed:** Volatility, excess, and fractured harmony. You are swinging between starvation and gluttony, silence and screams. The mixture is curdling because your fire is too hot and your patience too thin. Step away from the crucible.

**proverb:** The finest medicine becomes a lethal poison when measured with a trembling hand.

## The Devil (`maj_15`)
- Means, upright: Attachment, compulsion, and the feeling of being trapped. The chains are looser than they look. (attachment, materialism, compulsion, feeling trapped, temptation)
- Means, reversed: Breaking free of a habit, a bond, or a belief that held you. Seeing the chain is the first step; lifting it is the second. (liberation, breaking free, awakening, reclaiming power, release)

**upright:** Horns in the shadows and iron chains hanging loosely around the neck. Notice the links, seeker—they are wide enough to slip right over your ears whenever you choose. The Devil thrives on your willing enslavement to appetite, fear, or golden cages. Name your poison before it names you.

**reversed:** The chain slips from the collarbones. You are waking from the stupor, blinking in the gray light, realizing the monster was just a shadow cast by your own unresolved hunger. Break the talisman and walk out of the cellar.

**proverb:** The heaviest chains are always the ones we forge out of our own secret appetites.

## The Tower (`maj_16`)
- Means, upright: Sudden upheaval that brings down what was built on a false footing. It is painful, and it clears the ground. (upheaval, revelation, collapse, breakthrough, hard truth)
- Means, reversed: Disaster averted, or disaster denied: the structure is cracking and you are not looking. Face the fault before it faces you. (disaster averted, denial, fear of change, delaying the inevitable, a slow collapse)

**upright:** A flash of white lightning through the stained glass! The crown tumbles, the masonry splits, and the illusions come crashing down. Yes, it stings; yes, the dust will choke you. But that fortress was built on a foundation of lies, darling. Rejoice that the roof is gone—now you can see the stars again.

**reversed:** A delayed disaster or narrowly escaping a catastrophe only to rebuild the same fragile wall. Do not try to shore up crumbling mortar with wishful thinking. Let the rotten timber fall before it crushes your children.

**proverb:** Lightning strikes only the tallest arrogance; humility walks through the courtyard untouched.

## The Star (`maj_17`)
- Means, upright: Hope, healing, and renewed faith after a hard stretch. Look up; the way is clearer than it was. (hope, inspiration, serenity, healing, faith)
- Means, reversed: Despair, cynicism, or lost faith in your own gifts. The stars have not gone; you have stopped looking for them. (despair, disillusionment, cynicism, lost faith, discouragement)

**upright:** Ah, pour the tea and let out the long breath you have held for three seasons. The Star rises over the scorched earth. Hope, pure and unsullied, returns to the marrow. The spring water flows freely; your dignity is restored. Trust the quiet blessing that is settling over your shoulders.

**reversed:** Despair, cynicism, and throwing dirt into your own well. Because you were burned once, you claim the stars are dead. Bitterness is the armor of the terrified. Lay down your cynicism; the heavens have not abandoned you.

**proverb:** Even the darkest midnight is merely the canvas upon which the stars sharpen their light.

## The Moon (`maj_18`)
- Means, upright: Illusion, fear, and the pull of the subconscious. Not everything is what it seems; move slowly and trust your instincts. (illusion, intuition, fear, dreams, uncertainty)
- Means, reversed: The fog is lifting and the fears are shrinking. What frightened you is smaller in daylight. (clarity, truth revealed, fear released, understanding, calm)

**upright:** The wild dog howling at the pond while the crayfish crawls from the mud. Illusions, mirages, nightmares, and subconscious tides. Not everything is as it appears in this silver twilight. Do not sign contracts or swear oaths while the moon is drowning in the fog; walk slowly and test every stone with your staff.

**reversed:** The fog begins to thin under the dawn. Truths once obscured in murky waters surface. Deceptions are unmasked, and the phantom terror shrinks back to its true size: just an old coat hanging on a nail.

**proverb:** In moonlight, even a thistle casts the shadow of a spear; wait for the sun before you raise your shield.

## The Sun (`maj_19`)
- Means, upright: Joy, vitality, and success in plain sight. Enjoy a good day for what it is. (joy, vitality, success, clarity, warmth)
- Means, reversed: A clouded or muted joy, unrealistic optimism, or burnout from too much of a good thing. The sun is still there behind the mist. (clouded joy, muted enthusiasm, unrealistic expectations, burnout, delay)

**upright:** Radiant, golden, unapologetic triumph! The child upon the white horse under sunflowers taller than a man. Warmth fills every cold corner of your life. Success, vitality, clarity, and unashamed joy. Stand in the open square and let the light wash away every cobweb.

**reversed:** A veiled sun or overconfidence bordering on sunstroke. You are either having trouble accepting genuine happiness because you expect the other shoe to drop, or you are behaving with insufferable arrogance. Temper your glare with warmth.

**proverb:** The sun never asks permission to shine; it simply warms everything brave enough to look up.

## Judgement (`maj_20`)
- Means, upright: A reckoning and a calling. Take stock of what you have done and answer the summons to change. (reckoning, awakening, a calling, forgiveness, renewal)
- Means, reversed: Harsh self-judgment, ignoring the call, or guilt that keeps you from moving. Forgive what needs forgiving and get up. (self-doubt, harsh judgment, ignoring the call, guilt, hesitation)

**upright:** The silver trumpet sounds over the open graves! Stand up and shed your shroud, seeker. You are being summoned to your true vocation, your reckoning, your rebirth. Forgive the old sins, leave the cemetery behind, and answer the bell. This is your absolution.

**reversed:** Self-recrimination, guilt, and ducking back into the coffin because the trumpet is too loud. You are holding court against yourself and acting as corrupt judge and weeping prisoner. Pardon yourself and step into the daylight.

**proverb:** The trumpet sounds to awaken you, not to condemn you; why do you keep polishing your guilt?

## The World (`maj_21`)
- Means, upright: Completion and wholeness. A cycle has finished; celebrate it before the next one begins. (completion, wholeness, achievement, integration, fulfillment)
- Means, reversed: Unfinished business, shortcuts, or a delay at the final step. Finish the last mile. (incompleteness, unfinished business, delay, emptiness, shortcuts)

**upright:** The circle is complete. Four guardians at the four corners, and the dancer in the laurel wreath. An entire epoch of your life has reached fulfillment, harmony, and mastery. Savor this wholeness; you have walked the full circle and arrived back at your true name.

**reversed:** An unfinished symphony. You are at the ninety-ninth step and sitting on the stair complaining of fatigue. Tie up the loose ribbons, close the book, and finish what you started before starting a new chapter.

**proverb:** Completion is the doorway where endings and beginnings clasp hands in silence.

## Ace of Wands (`wands_ace`)
- Means, upright: A new idea, project, or desire has real energy behind it. Start while the spark is hot rather than waiting to feel ready. (inspiration, a creative spark, potential, initiative)
- Means, reversed: You want to begin but cannot find the push, or the push keeps getting spent on the wrong thing. Wait for the real spark, then act on it quickly. (hesitation, burnout, low energy, creative block)

**upright:** A dry branch bursting into wild flame! Raw creative voltage, an itch in the blood, an urgent inspiration that refuses to be ignored. Seize the brand while the embers are hungry; your will is a match struck in the dark.

**reversed:** A smothered ember or a spark blown into dry chaff. Delays, false starts, or creative energy turning inward into irritability and spite. Do not force the flame with bellows; give it dry kindling first.

**proverb:** A single spark can burn down a kingdom or light the hearth for winter; tend the fire with intention.

## Two of Wands (`wands_2`)
- Means, upright: You have done well enough to want more, and you are weighing where to go next. Plan the longer route and commit to one direction. (planning, ambition, a decision, the long view)
- Means, reversed: You are afraid to leave what you know, so the plan stays a plan. Either go, or admit you have chosen to stay. (fear of the unknown, playing it safe, bad timing, indecision)

**upright:** Standing on the battlements with the brass globe in your palm, looking out over the harbor. You have established your foundation; now the world beyond beckons. Will you stay in the safe fortress, or charter the ship into uncharted seas?

**reversed:** Fear of leaving the harbor, or restless daydreams that distract you from the work at hand. You are pacing the parapet until the stone is worn smooth. Make the decision, or the tides will make it for you.

**proverb:** Planning is the map; courage is the ship. Neither reaches the shore without the other.

## Three of Wands (`wands_3`)
- Means, upright: What you set in motion is starting to come back to you. Expect progress, news from a distance, and room to expand. (expansion, foresight, progress, first results)
- Means, reversed: Results are slower than you expected and the plan looks shakier than it did. Check what is actually in motion before you assume it has failed. (delays, frustration, obstacles, an unrealized plan)

**upright:** Your ships are returning, their sails swelling on the horizon. The seeds you planted with sweat and silence are bearing cargo. Expand your vision; what you built is solid enough to support greater ambition.

**reversed:** Shipwrecks at sea, or delays at port. Your expectations were premature or your supply lines neglected. Patience, seeker; do not curse the ocean because the cargo takes time to unload.

**proverb:** The merchant who paces the dock cannot hasten the wind; cultivate patience while the tide turns.

## Four of Wands (`wands_4`)
- Means, upright: Something is worth celebrating: a homecoming, a milestone, a place that finally feels settled. Enjoy it with the people who helped. (homecoming, celebration, community, stability)
- Means, reversed: Home, or the group you count on, feels unsettled, or a celebration has tension under it. Repair the foundation before you plan the party. (instability, family tension, a delayed return, insecurity)

**upright:** Garlands of ivy and roses strung between four carved wooden bower posts. Homecoming, celebratory hearths, community shelter, and a sacred milestone achieved. Rest your boots by the fire and drink with those who love you.

**reversed:** Domestic friction, a spoiled feast, or feeling like an alien in your own parlor. Tension under the rafters. Clean the hearth and mend the quiet grudges before inviting guests to table.

**proverb:** A home is not built of timber and mortar, but of the peace welcomed across the threshold.

## Five of Wands (`wands_5`)
- Means, upright: Competing wants, voices, or egos are pulling in different directions. Treat the friction as a workout rather than a war, and look for the useful idea in the noise. (competition, disagreement, friction, clashing egos)
- Means, reversed: The squabbling has either worn everyone out or is about to turn genuinely hostile. Step back from a fight that no longer has a point. (petty bickering, exhaustion, avoiding conflict, finding agreement)

**upright:** Five youths flailing staves in chaotic scrimmage. Rivalry, noisy competition, cross-purposes, and testing of mettle. It looks worse than it is—this is friction to sharpen your blade, not warfare to take your head.

**reversed:** Avoiding necessary confrontation until resentment festers into sabotage, or exhaustion from useless squabbling. Walk away from the brawl; not every contest deserves your sweat.

**proverb:** When five people shout at once, only the wind hears the argument.

## Six of Wands (`wands_6`)
- Means, upright: You have earned a win and other people can see it. Accept the recognition without apologizing for it. (victory, recognition, confidence, pride)
- Means, reversed: Recognition is late, missing, or hollow, and pride may be covering a private disappointment. Measure the win by what you did, not by the applause. (inflated ego, a fall from favor, hollow praise, lost standing)

**upright:** Laurel wreath on the staff and cheers along the cobbled street! Recognition, public triumph, and validation for battles fought in secret. Take your victory lap, but keep your horse humble.

**reversed:** Fall from grace, hollow applause, or betrayal by those who carried your banners yesterday. Do not hitch your self-worth to the fickle cheers of the town square.

**proverb:** The same crowd that scatters palms before your horse will gladly sweep up your ashes tomorrow.

## Seven of Wands (`wands_7`)
- Means, upright: You hold a position others are challenging, and you are right to defend it. Stand your ground, but pick the battles that matter. (standing your ground, conviction, defense, courage under pressure)
- Means, reversed: Defending everything has exhausted you, and some of what you are guarding is not worth the fight. Lower the guard where you can and rest. (feeling overwhelmed, exhaustion, giving in, defensiveness)

**upright:** Standing on high ground with six staves lunging up from the ravine below. You are outnumbered, but you hold the vantage point. Dig your heels in, swing wide, and do not cede an inch of your hard-won ground.

**reversed:** Exhaustion, crumbling resolve, or defending a hill that is no longer worth dying upon. Pick your battles wisely, darling; holding every barricade is how soldiers bleed to death.

**proverb:** Courage is not the absence of fear, but the refusal to yield high ground to loud shadows.

## Eight of Wands (`wands_8`)
- Means, upright: Things are moving fast and in your favor. Expect quick news and quick progress, and keep your attention on the direction rather than the speed. (momentum, quick news, fast progress, alignment)
- Means, reversed: Delays, crossed messages, and haste are scrambling what should have been quick. Slow down enough to read what is actually in front of you. (delays, haste, crossed messages, frustration)

**upright:** Eight arrows whistling through the azure sky! Swift news, rapid developments, lightning-fast messages, and events gathering unstoppable speed. Clear your desk and sharpen your quill; the post arrives at a gallop.

**reversed:** Misdirected arrows, panic, delays in communication, or acting in frantic haste without aiming. Slow down before you skewer your own foot.

**proverb:** An arrow loosed in panic strikes only regret; steady your breath before releasing the string.

## Nine of Wands (`wands_9`)
- Means, upright: You are tired and bruised but still standing, and the end is closer than it feels. Hold the line a little longer. (resilience, persistence, a last stand, endurance)
- Means, reversed: You are braced for a blow that may never come, and the vigilance is costing more than the danger would. Let the guard down where it is safe to. (exhaustion, hypervigilance, paranoia, walls too high)

**upright:** The wounded sentinel leaning on his staff, bandaged brow and vigilant eye. You have taken blows, and you are tired to the bone—yet you remain standing. One final perimeter check. You are stronger than your scars.

**reversed:** Paranoia, defensive exhaustion, and treating every knock on the door like a battering ram. You are guarding ruins against ghosts. Lower your guard enough to let someone bring you a bowl of broth.

**proverb:** Scars are proof that you healed, not orders to spend eternity in the watchtower.

## Ten of Wands (`wands_10`)
- Means, upright: You are carrying more than your share, and you are close to the finish. Set something down, or ask for help before you drop all of it. (burden, overcommitment, responsibility, nearly there)
- Means, reversed: You are either buckling under the load or finally putting some of it down. Delegate what you can and stop treating exhaustion as a virtue. (delegation, collapse, dropping the load, refusing martyrdom)

**upright:** Carrying ten heavy bundled logs uphill, back bent nearly double until you cannot see the roof of your own cottage. You took on everyone's burdens because you thought no one else could carry them. Drop four logs right here on the road, or you will collapse on your doorstep.

**reversed:** The load has collapsed into the ditch, or you are finally learning the holy art of saying 'No.' Refusing to carry other people's luggage is not abandonment; it is survival.

**proverb:** A martyr is just someone who mistook exhaustion for nobility.

## Page of Wands (`wands_page`)
- Means, upright: A new enthusiasm wants your attention: a message, a hobby, a bold idea. Follow it with curiosity and see where it points. (enthusiasm, curiosity, a new idea, adventure)
- Means, reversed: Enthusiasm is scattered or all talk, and nothing is being finished. Pick one spark and give it a week of real attention. (flightiness, procrastination, empty promises, impatience)

**upright:** A bright-eyed messenger in a feathered cap, leaning over a blossoming staff with news of adventure. A creative idea arrives, bursting with playful audacity. Say yes to the quest; the road wants you.

**reversed:** Petulance, temper tantrums, loud boasts followed by immediate retreat, or bad news. A spoiled spark that scorches the rug and runs away.

## Six of Pentacles (`pentacles_6`)
- Means, upright: Generosity flowing fairly, whether you are giving or receiving. Share without keeping score. (generosity, charity, fairness, support)
- Means, reversed: Charity with strings attached, or a power imbalance in who gives and who owes. Check what the generosity is really buying. (strings attached, power imbalance, inequity, debt)

**upright:** A wealthy merchant weighing coins on a balanced scale, giving generously to the kneeling needy. Generosity, charity, fair distribution of resources, reciprocal aid, and cosmic karmic balance. Give without condescension; receive without shame.

**reversed:** Strings attached to gifts, debt traps, patronizing charity, or extortion. Beware the benefactor who demands your soul in exchange for your bread.

**proverb:** True charity leaves the recipient's dignity intact; anything less is merely a bribe to vanity.

## Seven of Pentacles (`pentacles_7`)
- Means, upright: You have worked hard and are waiting for the harvest. Assess what is growing and be patient with what is not ready. (patience, assessment, long-term effort, perseverance)
- Means, reversed: Impatience or disappointment with a slow return. Decide whether to keep investing or redirect your effort. (impatience, wasted effort, giving up early, disappointment)

**upright:** A farmer leaning heavily on his hoe, contemplating seven golden disks growing on the vine. Assessment, patience, long-term investment, and the quiet realization that growth takes time. You have worked hard; let the vine do its quiet work.

**reversed:** Impatience, abandoned crops, or working tirelessly on a field that will never yield wheat. If the vine is barren, stop watering the dead roots.

**proverb:** You cannot hurry the ripening of the vine by pulling on the leaves.

## Eight of Pentacles (`pentacles_8`)
- Means, upright: Apprenticeship and craft: show up, do the work, and improve a little every day. Mastery comes through repetition. (apprenticeship, craft, dedication, diligence)
- Means, reversed: Perfectionism or tedium has drained the care out of the work. Reconnect with why you do it, or change what you do. (perfectionism, tedium, cutting corners, uninspired work)

**upright:** A blacksmith hammering detail into the eighth pentacle, with seven already completed and hung upon the post. Diligent apprenticeship, painstaking craft, honing technique, and devotion to mastery. Lose yourself in the beauty of doing the work properly.

**reversed:** Monotonous drudgery, cutting corners, lack of passion, or perfectionism paralyzing production. Work done without pride is only slow decay.

**proverb:** Mastery is simply a thousand ordinary days remembered with devotion.

## Nine of Pentacles (`pentacles_9`)
- Means, upright: Self-sufficiency and earned comfort. Enjoy the independence you built. (self-reliance, comfort, independence, earned reward)
- Means, reversed: The security looks better than it feels, or it depends on someone else more than you admit. Be honest about the numbers and about the loneliness. (a gilded cage, vanity, dependence, loneliness in comfort)

**upright:** A noblewoman in a gilded gown strolling through her lush vineyard, a hooded falcon resting serenely on her gloved wrist. Self-sufficiency, refined luxury, solitary elegance, financial independence, and enjoying the fruits of your own labor without needing a patron.

**reversed:** Superficial vanity, living beyond your means, golden handcuffs, or loneliness at the center of the estate. All the silk in France will not keep a hollow heart warm.

**proverb:** True luxury is the quiet freedom to say 'I am content with what my own hands have tended.'

## Ten of Pentacles (`pentacles_10`)
- Means, upright: Lasting wealth, family, and legacy. Build something that outlives the moment, or let yourself belong to what already does. (legacy, lasting wealth, family, security)
- Means, reversed: Family or financial trouble, an inheritance dispute, or a tradition that has become a burden. Have the practical conversation. (family dispute, a crumbling legacy, financial burden, mismanagement)

**upright:** An elder patriarch in an embroidered coat petting hounds beneath the stone archway of his estate, with children and grandchildren prospering around him. Generational legacy, ancestral inheritance, enduring stability, and roots that anchor ten generations. Honor the bloodline.

**reversed:** Family feud over wills, crumbling estate, financial betrayal among kin, or clinging to obsolete family expectations. Do not let ancestral debts mortgage your children's joy.

**proverb:** A true legacy is not what you leave in the vault, but what you planted in the character of those who follow.

## Page of Pentacles (`pentacles_page`)
- Means, upright: A practical beginning: studying, saving, learning a skill. Ambition here is quiet and steady. (study, practical ambition, eagerness to learn, a first step)
- Means, reversed: Procrastination, or plans without any practice behind them. Start the unglamorous lesson. (procrastination, lack of progress, financial naivety, wasted potential)

**upright:** A studious youth standing in a blooming pasture, reverently holding a golden coin aloft as though studying the mystery of seeds. A student of the earth, financial apprenticeship, practical curiosity, and grounded ambition. Study the foundation well.

**reversed:** Procrastination, lack of follow-through, squandered talents, or materialistic obsession without the willingness to study. All talk of riches, no sweat in the garden.

**proverb:** Every oak tree began as a nut that held its ground.

## Knight of Pentacles (`pentacles_knight`)
- Means, upright: Reliable, patient, methodical progress. It is not glamorous, but it arrives on time. (reliability, endurance, duty, patience)
- Means, reversed: Stubbornness, workaholism, or a routine that has stopped leading anywhere. Change the part that is only habit. (stubbornness, workaholism, tunnel vision, rigidity)

**upright:** A sturdy knight upon a heavy plow-horse, stationary in the plowed field, contemplating a single golden disk. The most dependable worker in the deck. Methodical, unyielding, tenacious, and utterly reliable. Keep your head down and finish the furrow.

**reversed:** Stubborn obstinacy, mind-numbing boredom, penny-pinching, or becoming a beast of burden out of fear of change. Don't be so stubborn that you plow the road.

**proverb:** The tortoise does not mock the hare; he simply arrives at the finish line and goes to sleep.

## Queen of Pentacles (`pentacles_queen`)
- Means, upright: Practical care: a home that works, money handled well, warmth you can feel. Provide it, and keep some for yourself. (practical care, hospitality, common sense, comfort)
- Means, reversed: Caring for everyone has left you depleted, or security has become anxious control. Tend your own needs too. (self-neglect, smothering, status obsession, anxiety about security)

**upright:** A benevolent queen seated on a throne carved with cherubs and goats, cradling a golden pentacle in her lap while a wild hare leaps at her feet. Earth mother, practical wisdom, abundant hospitality, financial savvy, and creating sanctuary where body and soul flourish.

**reversed:** Smothering materialism, neglecting self-care while feeding everyone else, social climbing, or hoarding pantry jars while the spirit starves. Feed your own bones first, darling.

**proverb:** A warm kitchen and an honest ledger do more to heal the soul than a thousand cathedral prayers.

## King of Pentacles (`pentacles_king`)
- Means, upright: Steady abundance built with discipline. Share the method, not just the money. (abundance, discipline, security, generosity)
- Means, reversed: Greed, rigidity, or neglect of the material side of life. Fix the foundation before the floor gives way. (greed, stubbornness, corruption, neglect)

**upright:** A sovereign upon a throne overflowing with carved bulls, grapevines, and golden coins, velvet robe trailing in the grass. The master of the material realm: business acumen, generosity, steady security, and turning whatever he touches into gold. Rule your domain with grounded generosity.

**reversed:** Greed, corruption, stubborn materialism, judging human worth solely by bank balance, or financial collapse through reckless speculation. A miser dies in a room full of gold and leaves nothing behind but envy.

**proverb:** Wealth is a tool to build shelter and freedom; worship it, and it becomes a tomb.
